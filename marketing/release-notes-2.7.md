## Hinweise für die Einreichung

**Review-Notizen.** Hintergrund-Standort und Anzeigen wie in 2.6; neu sind die zwei
Pro-Produkte, der Wegfall des Caddys und die Driving Range.

> During a golf round the app can, if the user turns it on, record positions to derive tee and green positions and show the distance to the flag. A round takes four to five hours with the display mostly locked, which is why the location background mode is needed. Recording is off by default, runs only during a round and ends with it. The data does not leave the device.
> New in 2.7 – one premium offer, GolfTrack Pro: all audio training lessons in the Training tab and no ads. Three ways to buy it on one screen: GolfTrackPro_abo (monthly subscription, existing product), GolfTrackPro_abo_jahr (new yearly subscription, same group and level as the monthly one) and GolfTrackPro_lifetime (new non-consumable). To see the purchase screen: Profile → "GolfTrack Pro", or tap any locked lesson in the Training tab. The voice assistant "Caddy" and the separate Training/Caddy/ad-free products are no longer sold; earlier purchases of them keep unlocking Pro (or ad-free) in the app. The app no longer uses the microphone.
> Driving range: home screen → "Driving Range". Pick a club, enter a distance, compare with earlier sessions. Eight new Game Center achievements and the leaderboard "longestDrive" belong to this feature.
> The ads on the scorecard do not come from an ad network: venue operators book them directly with the developer and the app loads them as a plain list from golftrack.app. No ad SDK, no device identifier, no IDFA, no profiling. Only ad ID, event type (impression or tap) and a count are reported, aggregated and not linked to a user. GolfTrack Pro removes them.
